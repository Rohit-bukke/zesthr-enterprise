1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================
ZESTHR-ENTERPRISE (internally termed HROS) is an enterprise-grade Human Resource 
Management System (HRMS) and Human Resource Information System (HRIS) designed
specifically for medium-to-large multi-entity organizations.

Unlike simple attendance-punch or billing scripts, the platform is architected 
around an immutable, tenant-scoped, audit-trailed domain model.

================================================================================
3. ARCHITECTURE & MULTI-TENANCY FOUNDATION
================================================================================
- Modular Django Monolith: Business logic is partitioned cleanly by domain under
  `core/` and `apps/organizations/`.
- Multi-Tenancy Engine:
  * Tenant isolation is enforced at the queryset level via `scope_queryset()`.
  * Every major business model (`Employee`, `Attendance`, `LeaveApplication`,
    `PayrollRun`, `CompensationBand`, `ExpenseCategory`) owns a strict foreign
    key to `organizations.Organization`.
  * Cross-tenant access is structurally prevented: selecting a department,
    component, or employee from another tenant raises an immediate `ValidationError`.
  * Organization hierarchy supports Companies, Branches, Departments, Teams,
    Cost Centers, and Job Grades.
- Immutability Architecture:
  * Historical records subclass `AppendOnlyModel`.
  * Modification or deletion of audit logs, attendance edits, leave decisions,
    and payroll events is strictly rejected at the application and DB trigger level.

4.5. Reward: Core Gross-to-Net Payroll & Payslip Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STATUS: 90% IMPLEMENTED - FULLY OPERATIONAL]
- Calculation Engine (`core/services/payroll.py`):
  * Exact monetary calculations using Python `Decimal` and cent rounding (`ROUND_HALF_UP`).
  * Per-day salary computation: `Monthly Basic Salary / Calendar Days in Period`.
  * Absence deductions calculated for absent days and half-days.
  * Recurring components: Dynamic earnings and deductions with effective date ranges.
  * One-off payroll adjustments with manager approval.
- Payroll Run Workflow (`PayrollRun`):
  * Multi-state lifecycle: `DRAFT` -> `SUBMITTED` -> `APPROVED` -> `FINALIZED` -> `REVERSED`.
  * Independent dual-approval requirement: Submitter cannot approve their own run.
  * Frozen Snapshots: Finalized runs persist a full JSON `calculation_snapshot`.
    Future salary changes will NEVER modify finalized historical payslips.
- Payslip Generation (`slip.html`):
  * Itemized salary slips with company details, attendance counts, earnings, and deductions.
  * Masked bank account numbers and statutory compliance identification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.6. Reward: Statutory Compliance Engine (EPF, ESIC, PT, TDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STATUS: 95% IMPLEMENTED - FULLY OPERATIONAL]
- Organization Statutory Settings (`OrganizationStatutoryConfig`):
  * Toggle EPF, ESIC, and PT compliance per organization.
  * Configurable wage ceilings (Default ₹15,000 for EPF) and contribution rates.
- Employee Statutory Profile (`EmployeeStatutoryProfile`):
  * Validated PAN (10-char uppercase alphanumeric regex).
  * Validated UAN (12-digit numeric regex) and ESIC IP numbers.
  * Voluntary PF contributions and custom TDS withholding amounts.
- Deduction Algorithms (`core/services/statutory.py`):
  * EPF: 12% employee contribution + 12% employer contribution (split into 3.67% EPF + 8.33% EPS + 0.5% EDLI).
  * ESIC: 0.75% employee contribution + 3.25% employer contribution for gross <= ₹21,000.
  * Professional Tax (PT): Multi-state slab calculators for Maharashtra (with February ₹300 spike),
    Karnataka, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, and West Bengal.
  * Full Employer CTC computation for true employment cost transparency.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STATUS: 90% IMPLEMENTED - FULLY OPERATIONAL]
- Category Configuration (`ExpenseCategory`):
  * Travel & Conveyance, Meals, Remote Office Equipment, Training & Certifications.
  * Category-level maximum limits and mandatory receipt attachment toggles.
- Reimbursement Lifecycle (`ExpenseClaim`):
  * Employee claim filing with title, amount, incurred date, and receipt file upload.
  * Manager / HR review queue with one-click Approve / Reject decisions.
- Payroll Integration:
  * Approved, unpaid claims automatically pull into monthly payroll calculations as
    NON-TAXABLE reimbursements added to Net Pay:
    Net Pay = (Gross Pay - Deductions) + Approved Expense Reimbursements.
  * Finalizing a payroll run links claims to the `PayrollRun` and marks them `PAID`.
  * Reversing a payroll run restores claims back to `APPROVED`.
